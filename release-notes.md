## ✨ New Features

- **Raid Kill Auto-Detect (preview)** — Kill Tracker → 🎯 **Auto-Detect** watches for each `RAIDTICK` and works out which boss died from enrage, slain, and tash/malo/slow messages, including the time of death. Watch-only for now: it suggests kills and never changes your sessions.
- **Manual prices in Networth** — click ✎ on any item to set your own price, including items with no WTS data. **Use market price** puts it back.
- **What's New screen** — shows once after each update (reopen any time from the top bar), with one-click buttons to suggest a feature or report a bug.
- **Browse suggestions** — the Feedback window now lists what everyone has already suggested, with search, so ideas don't get posted twice. (Only the subject, message and date are shown — never who sent it.)

## 🔧 Bug Fixes

- **Sessions auto-start for every character** — login, XP, kill and loot tracking now works for all your characters on a fresh install, not just ones that had exported their inventory on that PC.
- **Text boxes no longer freeze** — after a pop-up message, text boxes could stop accepting typing until you clicked away. Fixed everywhere in the app.
- **Gear Planner: Rogue & Monk weapons** — daggers tagged for the Range slot no longer rank too high in Main/Off Hand.
- **Gear Test Bench** — the stat breakdown now adds up to the weighted score.
